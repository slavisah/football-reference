# Editorial Guide

## Audience

Children, parents, casual fans, collectors, and readers discovering football history.

## Tone

- clear;
- curious;
- neutral;
- never mocking teams or players;
- brief explanations before statistics.

## Terminology

- Use **runner-up**, not “second winner”.
- Use **a.e.t.** only after explaining it means “after extra time”.
- Use **pens** only after explaining it means a penalty shoot-out.
- For EURO from 1984 onward, call both eliminated teams **semifinalists**.
- For the World Cup, use **third** and **fourth** because a third-place match is played.
- Preserve historical team names in edition tables.

## Content safety and family suitability

Do not include:

- betting odds or gambling links;
- graphic descriptions of crowd disasters or violence;
- abusive chants;
- unverified scandals;
- invasive details about players' private lives.

Difficult historical subjects may be included in a calm, age-appropriate context.

## Images

Prefer:

- original illustrations;
- maps created from open geographic data;
- public-domain or properly licensed images;
- country flags from an attribution-compatible source.

Do not download official tournament logos or copyrighted editorial photography without permission.
