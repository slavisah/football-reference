# The Ultimate Football Reference

Football history is easier to understand when tournaments, champions, hosts, finalists, and great individual achievements can be compared in one place.

This reference covers four major international competitions and two famous individual scoring or player awards:

1. FIFA World Cup
2. UEFA European Championship
3. Copa América
4. UEFA Nations League
5. Men's Ballon d'Or
6. FIFA World Cup and UEFA EURO top scorers

## How to use the reference

Each competition page contains:

- a concise introduction;
- a complete or near-complete edition-by-edition table;
- historical notes explaining format changes;
- a champions summary;
- memorable facts;
- links to primary sources.

## Important historical naming note

National teams and states have changed over time. This project generally records the name used during the tournament, while summary statistics may group sporting successors where that is conventional. Examples include West Germany/Germany, Soviet Union/Russia, and Czechoslovakia/Czechia.

The website should explain these choices rather than pretending that political and sporting continuity is always simple.
