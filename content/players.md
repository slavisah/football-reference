# Players

Every player who has won a Men's Ballon d'Or, or a FIFA World Cup or UEFA EURO Golden Boot, generated from the same award tables as the "Ballon d'Or" and "Golden Boot" pages - listing each player's full year-by-year award record in one place rather than only the per-competition winners tables. National teams are not included here; see "Teams" for that directory.
