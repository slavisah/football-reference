# Compare National Teams

Pick two national teams to compare their record across the FIFA World Cup, UEFA EURO, Copa América and UEFA Nations League - titles, runner-up finishes, and third/fourth-place or semifinal finishes, generated straight from each competition's edition table. Individual awards (Ballon d'Or, Golden Boot) are not included here since they recognize players, not national teams.
