# National Teams

Every national team that has won, been runner-up, or reached a tracked third/fourth-place or semifinal finish in the FIFA World Cup, UEFA EURO, Copa América, or UEFA Nations League - generated from the same edition tables as every competition page, listing each team's full year-by-year record in one place rather than only the aggregate counts shown on the "Compare National Teams" ranking. Individual awards (Ballon d'Or, Golden Boot) are not included here since they recognize players, not national teams.
