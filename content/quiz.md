# Family Quiz

Test what you know about football history with a quiz generated straight
from the competition tables on this site - champions, hosts, finals and top
scorers from the FIFA World Cup, UEFA EURO, Copa América, UEFA Nations
League, Ballon d'Or and Golden Boot. Nothing here is hand-typed trivia, so
the quiz stays in sync as new editions are added.

## How it works

- Pick an answer, then press "Check answer" to see if you got it right and
  add it to your score.
- Open "Just show me the answer" on any question to peek without playing.
- Press "Restart quiz" to clear your answers and play again.
- The "Champion order challenge" further down works differently: rank each
  champion with its dropdown, then press "Check order".

## Question types in this quiz

- Who won a given tournament in a given year?
- Which country hosted a given tournament?
- Who did the champion beat in the final?
- Who was the tournament's top scorer (Golden Boot)?
- Who won the Ballon d'Or in a given year?
- In which year did a given Ballon d'Or winner win it?
- Which team or player has won the most titles/awards in a competition
  overall?
- Put four champions from a tournament in chronological order (earliest
  first).
